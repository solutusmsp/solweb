MOUND, MINNESOTA · ESTABLISHED 2020

A small business.
A personal commitment.

Solutus LLC is a one-person company based in the western suburbs of Minneapolis–St. Paul, with a network of professionals available when a project calls for additional resources.

We specialize in mobile and web applications for small and medium-sized businesses, helping improve automation and access to information. We also build static and dynamic websites for personal and professional use.

You work directly with Cory, from the first conversation through the build and launch. That means personal attention, clear communication, and guidance tailored to your goals, budget, and comfort with technology. Decisions about how your site or app will be hosted, released, and maintained are part of the conversation from the start.
